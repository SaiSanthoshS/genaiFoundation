## General Disaster Awareness
- Stay informed through official alerts, weather updates, and local emergency channels.
- Keep a basic emergency kit ready with water, flashlight, batteries, medications, chargers, and copies of important documents.
- Know your evacuation routes and safe meeting places for your household.
- Make a family communication plan and keep emergency contacts easily accessible.
- If you are told to evacuate, leave early rather than waiting for conditions to worsen.

## Earthquake
- If you are indoors, drop, cover, and hold on under a sturdy table or desk.
- Stay away from windows, mirrors, and falling objects.
- If you are outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings, trees, and power lines.
- After shaking stops, check for injuries, avoid damaged buildings, and use stairs rather than elevators.

## Wildfire
- Watch for smoke, wind changes, and evacuation orders.
- Prepare a go-bag with masks, water, medicines, documents, and phone chargers.
- Keep a defensible space around your home by clearing dry leaves and brush.
- If evacuating, leave immediately and do not return until authorities say it is safe.

## Severe Storm
- Monitor local weather alerts and seek shelter before the storm arrives.
- Avoid windows, open fields, and isolated trees during lightning.
- Keep flashlights and charged devices ready in case power is lost.
- If flooding is possible, move to higher ground and avoid roads that may become submerged.

## Flood
- Move to higher ground quickly if floodwater begins to rise.
- Never walk or drive through moving water; even shallow water can be dangerous.
- Keep essential documents and valuables in waterproof bags.
- Turn off utilities if authorities instruct you to do so.

## Landslide
- Be alert for cracks in the ground, shifting soil, or unusual sounds from slopes.
- Move away from steep hillsides and avoid blocked roads or debris paths.
- If you notice danger signs, evacuate early and follow local warning messages.

## Drought
- Conserve water and follow local restrictions.
- Store drinking water and keep household supplies ready.
- Be aware of fire risk and avoid activities that could spark fires.

## Volcano
- Follow evacuation orders and stay away from valleys and low-lying areas.
- Wear protective masks if ashfall is possible.
- Keep emergency supplies ready because ash can disrupt travel and power.

## Tsunami
- If a tsunami warning is issued, move immediately to higher ground or inland.
- Stay away from beaches and harbors until officials confirm it is safe.
- Keep a communication plan and a go-bag ready for sudden evacuation.
